# SafeBelt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

SafeBelt for Windows ("SafeBelt") is made by an independent developer in Oman ("we"). It helps you check messages and links for scam warning signs, encrypt and decrypt private text, test passwords, and learn about scams.

## What SafeBelt does with your data
- **Everything runs on your computer.** Messages, links, passwords and passphrases you type are processed in memory on your device and are not sent anywhere.
- **No account, no tracking.** SafeBelt has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
- **No internet connection is used.** The app does not contact any server on the internet. The background connections built into the Chromium engine that SafeBelt runs on (spell-check dictionary downloads, component updates and similar) are switched off as well. If you press a button that opens a website (such as the store page of the SafeBelt browser extension), it opens in your own web browser, not in SafeBelt.
- **Clipboard.** SafeBelt reads the clipboard only when you paste (with a Paste button, the right-click menu or Ctrl+V) or press one of its keyboard shortcuts (by default Ctrl+Alt+F8, Ctrl+Alt+F9 and Ctrl+Alt+F10). The text is processed in memory and not stored. If you turn it on, passphrases and decrypted messages you copy from SafeBelt are wiped from the clipboard after the time you choose.
- **Your profile.** The first time SafeBelt opens, it asks for a name and a colour, and optionally a PIN. This profile is stored only on your PC, in the app's own data folder. The PIN itself is never stored: only a one-way scrambled form of it (a salted PBKDF2 hash) that is used to check it. If you turn on Windows Hello, Windows checks your face, fingerprint or Windows PIN and only tells SafeBelt yes or no. SafeBelt never sees or stores your face or fingerprint. There is no online account. You can remove your profile and progress from your PC at any time with **Settings > Remove profile**, or with "Forgot PIN" on the lock screen.
- **Recovery key, backups and moving to a new PC.** SafeBelt can make a recovery key, which is shown to you once and not stored (only a check value is kept). Backups (.safebelt-backup files) are saved only where you choose, such as a folder on your PC, a USB drive or your own OneDrive folder. They are encrypted with your recovery key and cannot be opened without it. SafeBelt does not upload them anywhere. When you send your profile to another PC, it goes directly over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted with a one-time code, and the sending stops after 10 minutes or once the new PC has it.
- **What is saved on your device.** Only your settings (shortcuts, theme, language, popup and clipboard options), simple counters and progress (how many messages you checked, scams you spotted, files you locked, which days you used the app for the safety streak, and badges earned), which quiz questions you have answered, and which steps you ticked in the "I think I was scammed" guide. These stay in the app's local storage on your computer. **Settings > Remove profile** deletes the counters and progress (your settings stay), and uninstalling SafeBelt removes everything.
- **Emails.** Email files you check, or email text you
